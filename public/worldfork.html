worldfork

james gui · built with hanson wen · april 2026

a multi-agent simulation project that explores several possible futures from the same starting scenario. it won the listen labs “simulate humanity” track at hacktech by caltech.

what i worked on

my hackathon contribution was the frontend and creating branches. hanson worked on the backend engine and cli. our devpost submission documents the original build.

how it works

  1. define the starting scenario, actors, constraints, and decisions.
  2. simulate forward and branch at consequential decision points.
  3. compare timelines using their recorded state, assumptions, and reasoning.

the open-source project uses monte carlo tree search to explore those alternatives. the useful output is an inspectable set of possibilities, with a record of how each one developed.

from project to research

hanson and i coauthored from forecast scores to auditable benchmarks: worldfork for llm forecasting evaluation, part of our icml workshop work.

the paper examines how forecast evaluation can expose assumptions, unresolved uncertainty, and evidence provenance. its 24-case retrospective pilot is exploratory; it does not establish a general forecasting advantage.
