codexprophet

james gui · built with hanson wen · may 2026

a forecasting agent built on codex. we won the forecasting track at prophethacks, and the project led to an oral presentation at the icml forecasting workshop.

the problem

a prediction-market price is a useful starting point. the harder question is when new evidence justifies changing it.

the approach

  1. route each question to a domain-specific skill: sports, finance, entertainment, and more.
  2. gather market and primary-source evidence, then check the event’s resolution rules.
  3. make bounded probability adjustments and validate the final output.

we used codex’s existing agent loop, with skill files and historical calibration notes guiding the process.

the result

1st place in the forecasting track. the evaluation measured forecast accuracy over two weeks; it was not a claim of trading profitability.
