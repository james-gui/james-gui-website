codexprophet

james gui · built with hanson wen · may 2026

hanson wen and i built codexprophet to forecast events on kalshi. it starts with the market price and checks whether fresh evidence warrants changing the probability. the project earned an oral presentation at the icml 2026 forecasting workshop after winning the prophethacks forecasting track.

the problem

a prediction-market price is a useful starting point. the harder question is when new evidence justifies changing it.

the approach

  1. route each question to a domain-specific skill: sports, finance, entertainment, and more.
  2. gather market and primary-source evidence, then check the event’s resolution rules.
  3. make bounded probability adjustments and validate the final output.

we used codex’s existing agent loop and wrote a skill for each category. for a weather question, the agent checks which station will settle the market. for a sports question, it checks whether the odds already reflect a lineup change.

the result

prophet arena scored the forecasts against resolved kalshi events from may 17–31, 2026. codexprophet placed first, beating the market baseline by 0.0369 on brier score. the score measures how close a predicted probability comes to the actual outcome.
